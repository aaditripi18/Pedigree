Check a model's declared base

Compares a model's weights with its declared base_model, or a base you name, in this page. It reads file headers and the first 8,192 values of up to 24 matched tensors from huggingface.co; nothing is sent anywhere else.

How to read the verdict

  • RELATED median cosine above 0.02554 (the published threshold)
  • UNRELATED at or below it, with at least 8 tensors compared
  • NOT COMPARABLE fewer than 8 shared tensors: never read as unrelated

Safetensors models only. For PyTorch, GGUF and MLX files, gated models and the permutation-invariant check, use the CLI: pedigree verify hf:org/model.
