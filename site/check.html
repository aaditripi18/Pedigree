Check a model's declared base

Compares a model's weights with its declared base_model (or a base you name) in this page. It reads file headers and the first 8,192 values of up to 24 matched tensors from huggingface.co; nothing is sent anywhere else.

Safetensors models only; for PyTorch, GGUF and MLX files, gated models and the permutation-invariant check, use the CLI.
